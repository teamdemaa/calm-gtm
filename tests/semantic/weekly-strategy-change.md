# Weekly strategic-change contract

This is the positive counterpart to RelayCert's weak-signal week.

Given an agreed strategy aimed at owners of 5–30-technician companies, a later
week reports that 8 of 10 qualified buying conversations independently identify
the operations director in 30–100-technician companies as the budget owner,
describe the same urgent delay, and 4 accept the measured paid pilot.

Expected behavior:

- treat the repeated buyer, segment, pain, and paid-pilot evidence as material;
- change only the justified Positioning and Offer sections;
- preserve Alignment and avoid broad Promotion changes without channel data;
- include `Strategy changes` in that week's `weekly.md`, with the previous
  hypothesis, new hypothesis, and the exact evidence;
- set `APOP Changes` in `weekly.csv` to `PO1|PO2|OF3`: the repeated evidence
  changes the priority segment and buyer, strengthens the important problem
  from hypothesis to known, and validates willingness to buy the priced pilot;
- preserve `PO3`, `OF1`, and `OF2` because this fixture does not provide new
  evidence about the current alternative, the promised outcome, or the
  contents of the pilot;
- append exactly 12 new rows to `strategy.csv` for the material revision, using
  a unique ISO-8601 `Period` later than the prior revision;
- update affected future actions and dependent assets, preserving history;
- never rewrite prior weekly entries.
